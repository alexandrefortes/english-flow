# Negotiate a deadline

The Doist conversation is about an illustration. A colleague proposes Thursday instead of Monday. The other colleague suggests the end of Wednesday to allow time for changes. [Original Doist publication](https://www.todoist.com/inspiration/remote-team-communication-tools#todoist)

Open `doist-combinar-prazo` or its sample alias `prazo-imagem-todoist` before quoting the source. The dialogue below is a **British English adaptation**.

## First attempt

The learner is preparing a report. You need it on Monday. Finance can send the final figures on Tuesday morning. The learner can deliver the draft by 4 pm on Wednesday. You need time to read it before Thursday's meeting.

1. Start as the colleague.

   > Could you send me the report by Monday?

2. For a hint, offer *Would Wednesday …?*
3. After the proposal, ask about the point that still needs agreement. Use this response if you need part of the report earlier.

   > Could you send me the draft by lunchtime on Wednesday? I'd like some time to review it before Thursday's meeting.

4. Give the model after the attempt, or earlier if requested.

   > Would Wednesday at 4 pm work? Finance can send the final figures on Tuesday morning. If you need something by lunchtime on Wednesday, I can send the sections that are ready and add the remaining figures by 4 pm.
   >
   > Just to confirm, I'll send you the main sections by lunchtime on Wednesday and the full draft by 4 pm.

## Feedback

Check for a workable date, a reason, and agreement on who will deliver what. Other dates can work. Explain one useful difference. For example, *by 4 pm* sets a deadline. *Until 4 pm* says when an activity ends. Ask for a new confirmation with a different date.

## Practice on another day

A supplier expects signed approval by Friday. The learner's manager returns on Monday. The learner can send an update on Friday. The signed approval is due on Tuesday at 11 am.

1. Start as the supplier. Wait before you show a model.

   > Will you be able to send over the signed approval by Friday?

2. Check that the reply distinguishes the Friday update from the Tuesday approval.
3. After the attempt, offer a model.

   > Could we move the deadline for the signed approval to Tuesday at 11 am? Our manager is back on Monday. I'll send you an update by Friday and confirm the delivery time once the approval is signed.
