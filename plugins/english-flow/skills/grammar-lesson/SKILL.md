# Teach a grammar lesson

First [select the study environment](../notebook-review/SKILL.md#select-the-study-environment). Use that wiki and notebook throughout the lesson.

Start the conversation in English. If the learner asks for another language, use it for explanations, instructions, and feedback. Keep the study material in English. Follow the [conversation language procedure](../notebook-review/SKILL.md#conversation-language).

1. To continue a series, list the lesson titles in the selected notebook.
2. Choose the work task that the rule supports.
3. Find an original source for that task. [Consult a source](../notebook-review/SKILL.md#consult-a-source)
4. Explain who is speaking, who listens, and why the speaker uses the sentence.
5. Show the original English sentence. Explain one rule at a time at the pace the learner requests.
6. Give one English example that shows the effect of a change. For example, move the subject in an indirect question.

For a repeat lesson, use the same topic, source, and notebook entry. End a lesson after the explanation. Give an exercise if the learner asks for practice.

For a new lesson, write the title, objective, rule, and examples in English. Save it in the selected notebook with `kind: lesson` and the source IDs. Follow the [notebook save procedure](../notebook-review/SKILL.md#save-a-review-or-note).
